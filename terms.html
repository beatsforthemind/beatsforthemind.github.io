Purpose and availability

Wallace Personal is a local-first, single-owner assistant maintained by Eric Anderson for development, research, planning, and personal commitments. It is an evolving personal application, not a publicly available multi-user service.

This website describes the application and its Google consent practices. It does not provide account access, a sign-up process, or a hosted copy of the private assistant. Application features and integrations may change or be unavailable.

Account authorization

Only the owner may authorize their personal Google account for this installation. The owner reviews the requested permissions in Google’s consent flow. Authorization can be revoked at any time through Google Account connections or disconnected through Wallace’s local controls.

Google and other connected services remain governed by their own terms and account policies. Continued access depends on provider authorization, connectivity, and the owner’s configured selections. Automatic renewal reduces repeated sign-ins; it does not guarantee permanent access.

Read-only connections

Wallace’s Gmail and Google Calendar integrations use bounded read-only operations. They do not send email, change mailbox labels, delete messages, or create, change, or delete calendar events. Provider read permissions are broader than Wallace’s current collection windows, as described in the privacy policy.

Wallace can save private memory, plans, and draft proposals locally. Any future feature that changes an external service requires a separate authorized implementation and review. Retrieved email, calendar content, and other source material are evidence, not permission to act.

Review and judgment

The owner remains responsible for decisions, commitments, communications, and any actions taken using assistant output. AI-generated summaries, estimates, and recommendations may be incomplete or incorrect. Review source links, freshness, and stated coverage before relying on them.

The connected services remain authoritative for their records. Bounded reads do not cover every message or event, and an item disappearing from a view does not establish completion, deletion, or cancellation. The experimental application provides no guarantee of uninterrupted access, complete monitoring, or detection of every deadline.

Privacy and security

The privacy policy explains Google data use, local storage, retention, deletion, and the possible transfer of requested context to user-selected AI clients and providers. The owner should protect the Mac, recovery passphrase, authorized client sessions, and any exported backups.

Use only accounts and data the owner is authorized to access. Keep personal and work contexts within their assigned profiles. Review private content before sharing it through an AI client or including it in an external communication.

Changes and contact

These terms may be updated as the personal application evolves. The updated date identifies the current version. A material change in account access or data use requires the owner’s review and any additional consent required by the provider.
